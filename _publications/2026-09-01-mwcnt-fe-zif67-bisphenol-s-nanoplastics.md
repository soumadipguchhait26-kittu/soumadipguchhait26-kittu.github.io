Dissolved organic contaminants and nanoplastics often occur together in water, yet conventional treatment is usually designed to handle either molecules or particles, not both. This study reports a magnetic MWCNT@Fe-ZIF 67 nanoheterostructure, built by integrating Fe-modified ZIF 67 with functionalised multiwalled carbon nanotubes. The resulting defect-engineered composite, containing mixed-valent cobalt–iron oxide domains, showed stronger visible-light absorption, better charge separation and higher surface redox activity. This enabled concurrent photocatalytic degradation of bisphenol S and heteroaggregation-driven removal of nanoplastics from water.
